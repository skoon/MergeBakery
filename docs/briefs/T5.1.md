The prerequisites column uses row numbers; write the ids. An unlock is `{ "kind": "customer", "customerId": … }`, `{ "kind": "generator", "itemId": … }` or `{ "kind": "oven", "ovenId": … }`.

For every task:

- `spot`: where the task sits in the shop picture, as fractions of its width and height. Keep `x` in 0.1–0.9 and `y` in 0.15–0.85, and no two spots closer than 0.1 in both x and y. Put related tasks together: the window, sign, awning and window boxes near the top, the counter, stool, display case and café table in the middle, the tiles near the bottom.
- `beforeSpriteKey` and `afterSpriteKey`: `corner-shop-<id>-before` and `corner-shop-<id>-after`. The art doesn't exist yet; the asset loader falls back to a placeholder.
- `sceneId`: `null`. T5.5 adds dialogue.

Why these unlocks: the second Dairy Fridge and Flour Mills give the player a way to replace a generator they merged up, and the second Toaster Oven makes the Brick Oven upgrade (T4.3) reachable at all. Chapter 1 features only flour, dairy and croissant items: per the GDD its generators are the Flour Mill and Dairy Fridge, and the Hen Coop and Sugar Tin (with the Cookie and Cupcake they feed) arrive in Chapter 2. The Kitchen already hides a recipe until the player owns generators for its inputs, so no task here should unlock them.

The star total is 106. Whether that fits the GDD's 2–3 day target is the balancing pass's job (T-O3), not this task's.
