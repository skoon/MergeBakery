## The pack

- **Id `holidayfun`**, name "Holiday fun!", version 1.0.0, window Dec 1–25 (closes as Dec 26 begins). Harvest Supper ends Nov 30, so the windows touch but don't overlap.
- **Generator** Holiday Sleigh 🛷 (one tier, 20 charges, 10 min cooldown): peppermints 55%, clementines 45%.
- **Ingredient chains**
  - Peppermint: Peppermint 🍬 → Handful of peppermints 🍬🍬🍬 → Candy cane (drawn).
  - Clementine: Clementine 🍊 → Two clementines 🍊🍊 → Basket of clementines 🧺.
- **Baked chains**, one and two of each, as in Harvest Supper:
  - Gingerbread: Gingerbread man (drawn) → Two gingerbread men (drawn).
  - Clementine cake: Clementine cake 🍰 → Two clementine cakes 🍰🍰.
- **Recipes**, Chapter 1 inputs only (decision 1):
  - Gingerbread man = handful of peppermints + flour bag + cream jug (5 min). (It first took a butter pat; see decision 1.)
  - Clementine cake = basket of clementines + flour bag + milk bottle (4 min).
- **Orders**: up to 3 open, 1–3 items of tier 1–3 from the pack's ingredient and baked chains, 5 Season Points per item.
- **Rules** (`rules.ts`): `orderReward` makes a seasonal order of 3 items a Gift order, paying 50% more coins. It is Harvest Supper's Feast rule under a new name.
- **Track**: five milestones, the top two paying the keepsakes `holidayfun-star` "Tree-top Star" 🌟 and `holidayfun-gingerbread-house` "Golden Gingerbread House" 🏠. Points 75, 175, 300, 450 and 600, set from the simulator the T14.9 way (decision 3); rewards as Harvest Supper's.
- **Sell values and gems** as Harvest Supper: ingredients 2/4/8, baked 30/60, 5 gems for completing each ingredient or baked chain.
- **Tile colours** (the chains' `color`): Holiday Sleigh holly `#b23a3a`, Peppermints pale pink `#f3b3bd`, Clementines frosty blue `#a9cce3`, Gingerbread pine `#7fb48a`, Clementine cakes `#f2a65a`. Pale or cool behind the red-and-white cane, the orange fruit and the brown gingerbread, so they read.
- **Theme**
  - Colours: `--color-crust` holly red `#8f2d2d`, `--color-butter` gold `#f3c95a`, `--color-strawberry` berry red `#d9483b`, `--color-mint` pine `#8cc7a1`.
  - Music: a bright major-key loop on a sine wave at 96 bpm, C major, I–vi–IV–V, rooted at C5 (an octave above Chapter 1's loop, which has the same chords).
  - Invitation: "Holiday fun! is here! The harbor is strung with lights, and Grandma has her gingerbread cutters out. Join in?"
  - Intro, four lines:
    1. Narrator: "Snow on the harbor and lights in every window. Holiday fun! has come to town."
    2. Grandma (happy): "Out came my gingerbread cutters! Gingerbread men and clementine cake, just like when you were small."
    3. Grandma: "Tap the Holiday Sleigh for peppermints and clementines. An order of three treats is a Gift order, and folks pay half again for those."
    4. Grandma (happy): "Flour from the Mill and cream and milk from the Fridge, and we're ready to bake."

## Decisions (defaults approved by Scott Nov 13)

1. **Recipes use only Chapter 1 inputs.** A player can join right after the tutorial, so both treats are bakeable from day one, and T14.11's "Needs a …" never shows for this pack. Harvest Supper's pie stalled on butter pats in the simulator, because the bot merged them into butter blocks for croissants (T14.9 decision 4). If gingerbread stalls the same way, it takes a cream jug instead (still Chapter 1), and the brief records the change.

   **It did, so gingerbread takes a cream jug.** Six seeds through a probe (the window with a points ladder every 25): with a butter pat the bot ended the window on 730–1,050 points and had flat spells of up to four days; with a cream jug, on 855–1,675 points, more evenly. The cream jug is also a tier below the butter pat. Intro line 4 says cream to match.

2. **The tier 2 baked item is "Two gingerbread men", not a gingerbread house.** The keepsake is the Golden Gingerbread House, and an item with the same name would read as the same thing. The id `holidayfun-gingerbread-house` stays with the keepsake.
3. **The track comes from the simulator's slowest seed**, as in T14.9 decision 5. The fourth milestone (the star) should land about two-thirds of the way through the window, and the last (the gingerbread house) with days to spare. The window is 25 days against Harvest Supper's 19, so the points will likely go up.

   **Set to 75/175/300/450/600.** A first cut from the probe's seed 6 (1,030 points) was 100/200/350/550/850, but with that track seed 2 in `holidayfun.test.ts` ended the window on 675 points: joined, it was still finishing the base game when the window closed. (Seed 2's own probe run ended on 855; the bot's runs swing with every change to the rewards.) That run is the slowest seen, and the track comes from it: there the star (450) lands about day 24, two-thirds of the way through a window that runs from day 8 to day 32, and the house (600) about day 28. The points went up less than the longer window suggested, because the slowest run here is slower than Harvest Supper's. In `holidayfun.test.ts`'s three seeds with this track, the bot reaches the star on days 19–22 and the house on day 23.

4. **Drawings, not emoji, for three tiles.** No emoji shows a candy cane or a gingerbread man. Each is an `art/<key>.svg` on the placeholder tile, as for the T14.10 pumpkins. The pair shows two gingerbread men, per the GDD's plural rule.
5. **The simulator test is its own file.** `src/sim/holidayfun.test.ts` follows `season.test.ts`: it starts on Nov 24 (day 1), the window opens on day 8 (Dec 1) and closes as day 33 begins (Dec 26), with seeds 1–3, joined and not joined. The report calls three-item orders Gift orders. The simulator itself doesn't change: it already counts three-item seasonal orders on its own.
6. **The id stays `holidayfun`**, as Scott typed it; it prefixes every id in the pack.
7. **Gift orders are told, not shown**, as Feasts are (T14.9 decision 1): the counter shows an order's base coins, and the hook pays half again on delivery. Grandma's intro tells the player.
