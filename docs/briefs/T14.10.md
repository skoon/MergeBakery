# Task T14.10: Scott plays the season

Context: Rise & Shine Bakery, a browser merge game (TypeScript, Vite, PixiJS, Vitest).
Read first: `docs/briefs/T14.9.md` (the pack and its decisions), `docs/briefs/T14.8.md` (the season UI and dev tools), `seasons/thanksgiving/season.json`, `seasons/thanksgiving/rules.ts`.

Goal: Scott plays Harvest Supper from the invitation to the keepsake shelf and judges what the tests and the simulator can't: whether it's fun, whether a real player's pace fits the track, and how the text, colours, music and placeholder art come across. The outcome is one list of findings, each marked fix now, later, or fine. The fixes become their own task (T14.11).

Branch: none for the play. The findings list goes into `task.md` under T14.10.

- To play in the real window, T14.6–T14.9 have to reach `main`. The Pages deploy runs `npm run build` on every push to `main`, which builds the packs first. GitHub Actions sets `CI`, so `seasons/keepsakes.json` must be committed with the pack, or the build fails.

## Two passes

1. **Dev pass, now (about an hour).** Run `npm run dev`, which builds the packs first, and open `http://localhost:5173/MergeBakery/`. In the console, `bakery.season('thanksgiving')` opens the season now, though it still closes on Dec 1. `bakery.endSeason()` ends it early and shows the result card. `bakery.season(null)` returns to the calendar. `bakery.give(...)`, `bakery.load({...})` and `bakery.reset()` set up a test case quickly. The dev save in localhost is separate from the deployed one. This pass is for the first impression and the end, not the pace.
2. **The real window, from Nov 12.** Play on the deployed site with your real save, at your own pace, for as much of the window as you like. This is the pace test the bot can't stand in for. On a phone or the installed app, the pack appears on the first load after the window opens. Play one session offline after that: the pack's files are cached.

## What to judge

1. **First contact.** The new-season banner and the invitation's wording. "Join" and "Not now". Grandma's four intro lines: do they explain the Pumpkin Patch, Feasts and pecans well enough?
2. **Pace.** At the end of each day, note the day and your Season Points (the sheet shows them). For comparison, the bot reaches the wreath (350) 8–11 days after joining and the trophy (500) 10–15 days after. It plays faster than a person, so a real player should be slower. Do you finish with days to spare, or not at all?
3. **Seasonal orders.** Too hard, too easy, or about right? Do all three order slots ever fill with orders you can't finish, such as two pie pairs at once? If so, note what they asked for. Did you notice Feasts paying half again, given the counter shows only the base coins?
4. **Pecans.** About 1 Flour Mill tap in 11 drops a pecan while the season runs. Is that noticeable, and too rare or too common?
5. **The base game alongside.** Does the season crowd out regular orders, chapter tasks or board space? MegaBun events are paused while you're in the season.
6. **Look and sound.** Are the autumn colours readable everywhere they apply? How does the autumn music loop sound? Is the emoji placeholder art good enough for this year? Any sprite can be replaced by dropping a PNG at `seasons/thanksgiving/art/<key>.png`.
7. **The end.** The result card's points, coins for leftover seasonal items, and keepsakes. The keepsakes on the Bakery shelf. Colours and music return to normal, and MegaBun events come back.

## What changes cost

These are the settings to tune:

- **In `season.json`:** milestone points and rewards, `pointsPerItem`, order count and size (`maxOpen`, `minItems`/`maxItems`, `minTier`/`maxTier`), the patch's charges, cooldown and spawn weights, and recipe inputs and bake times.
- **In `rules.ts`:** `PECAN_WEIGHT` and `FEAST_COINS`.

`npm run build:seasons` checks every change. `src/sim/season.test.ts` reruns the bot over the window.

Once a version is deployed, any change to the pack must raise its `version`. Pack files are cached by their versioned path, so an installed game would otherwise keep the old files. Players who have joined keep their points and claims. Claims are stored by milestone index, so don't reorder milestones or remove one mid-season. Keepsake sprite keys can never be dropped: the build refuses it.

## Decisions (Scott approved Nov 10)

1. **Both passes.** The dev pass first, so anything broken is fixed before the real window opens. Then the real window for pace.
2. **During the window, fix only blockers.** That means a broken rule, wrong text, or orders that can't be finished. Each fix goes out as a new version, 1.0.1 and up. Balance changes wait for the findings list unless the track is clearly out of reach.
3. **One findings list.** It goes in `task.md` under T14.10, each item marked fix now, later, or fine. T14.11 gets a brief from the "fix now" items.
