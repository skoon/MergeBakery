# Task T2.4: Five-item merge bonus

Context: Rise & Shine Bakery, a browser merge game (TypeScript, Vite, PixiJS, Vitest).
Read first: src/core/types.ts, src/core/drop.ts and src/core/drop.test.ts (T2.3), src/core/board.ts, src/core/locks.ts, src/core/level.ts, src/core/testing.ts, docs/briefs/working-rules.md.

Goal: Merging into a connected group of identical items that totals five gives two next-tier items instead of one.

Files to create or edit: src/core/drop.ts, src/core/drop.test.ts. Do not edit any other file.
Contract: `applyDrop`'s signature doesn't change. When a drop is a merge of item X from `from` onto `to`:

- The group is every cell orthogonally connected to `to` through cells holding X, including `to`, excluding `from`. Cobwebbed cells other than `to` don't count and don't connect.
- If the group has 4 or more cells (so 5 or more items counting the dragged one): consume the dragged item, `to`, and the 3 other group cells closest to `to` by straight-line distance between centers (ties go to the lowest index). Put a next-tier item at `to` and another at the consumed cell closest to `to`; the other consumed cells and `from` become empty. XP is twice a normal merge's. Clear adjacent locks around both result cells. The `merged` event's `cells` is `[to, <second cell>]`.
- If the group has 3 or fewer cells, it is a normal merge, unchanged.

Rules: pure functions in src/core; no edits to types.ts; follow docs/briefs/working-rules.md. Keep every existing drop test passing.

Done when: `npx vitest run src/core/drop.test.ts` passes, with new tests showing: 5 items (4 on the board plus the dragged one) give 2; 4 give the normal result; 6 leave one behind; diagonal items don't join the group; a cobwebbed item breaks the chain; locks open next to both results; XP doubles. The checks in working-rules.md pass.
If the contract seems wrong, stop and explain instead of changing it.
