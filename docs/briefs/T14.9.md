## The pack

- **Id `thanksgiving`**, name "Harvest Supper", version 1.0.0, window Nov 12–30. ("harvest" is taken: the base game's `harvest-showcase`.)
- **Generator** Pumpkin Patch 🎃 (one tier, 20 charges, 10 min cooldown): pumpkin sprouts 55%, cranberries 45%.
- **Chains**: Pumpkin (sprout → small pumpkin → pumpkin → prize pumpkin), Cranberry (cranberry → handful → basket), Pecan (pecan → bag → tin), and two baked chains, Pumpkin pie and Cranberry tart (one and two of each).
- **Recipes**: Pumpkin pie = pumpkin + flour bag + egg pair (5 min); Cranberry tart = handful of cranberries + flour bag + sugar bowl (4 min). (The pie first took a butter pat; see decision 4.)
- **Orders**: up to 3 open, 1–3 items of tier 1–3 from the pack's ingredient and baked chains, 5 Season Points per item.
- **Rules** (`rules.ts`): `extraSpawns` adds pecans (weight 10, about 1 tap in 11) to every Flour Mill's table; `orderReward` makes a seasonal order of 3 items a Feast order, paying 50% more coins.
- **Track**: five milestones at 50, 125, 225, 350 and 500 points, the top two paying keepsakes (Harvest Wreath, Prize Pumpkin Trophy). Points set from the simulator so a player of the bot's pace reaches the last one with days to spare, and the fourth in about two-thirds of the window (decision 5).
- **Theme**: warm orange and cranberry overrides of `--color-crust`, `--color-butter` and `--color-strawberry`; an autumn music loop in a minor key; Grandma's recipe-card intro; the invitation text.

## Decisions (defaults, recorded under /goal)

1. **Feast orders are told, not shown.** The counter shows an order's base coins; the hook pays the extra on delivery. Grandma's intro tells the player about Feasts and about pecans at the mill (the sheet's how-to is the same for every season). Showing the adjusted reward would need the hook in the UI, which has no rng to give it.
2. **Pecans are an order ingredient, not a recipe input.** The plan has two recipes; pecans reach the player only through the Flour Mill, so seasonal orders asking for them reward keeping a mill going.
3. **The simulator** gains `start` and `joinSeason` options. It starts on Nov 5 (a week before the window) from a new game; joining sets the tutorial done (the bot never runs the UI tutorial), tries `joinSeason` every step until it's open, and claims each milestone as it's reached. The report: points, the day each milestone was reached, keepsakes, coins from seasonal orders, and, joined against not joined with the same seeds, tasks done, the day the base game's last chapter was finished, level and coins.

   The bot also learns to play a season, as a player who joined would. Without this it never tapped the Pumpkin Patch (it ranked generators by their first spawn's chain alone, and pies count flour and eggs too, which always outranked pumpkins), and points stalled below 100. Now it ranks a generator by every chain it can spawn, the season's `extraSpawns` included (pecans make the Flour Mill count); it spends the season generator's charges first while an order needs what it spawns; and an unmet seasonal bake counts the recipe inputs still missing from the board (a pie pair needs two pumpkins). No base generator spawns from two chains and the other two changes only touch seasonal items, so the base game's runs are unchanged.

4. **The pie takes an egg pair, not a butter pat.** With the butter pat, pies stalled: the bot kept merging butter pats into butter blocks for croissants, and a cream jug stays held back for the cupcake and bonbon recipes. An egg pair costs about as much as a sugar bowl, which the tart already takes, and pumpkin, eggs and sugar are what a pumpkin pie's filling is made of anyway.
5. **The track is set from the slowest seed, not the median.** Across six seeds the bot finished the window with 585 to 1,660 points. The bot is a fast player, so the slowest seed stands in for a real one. There the wreath (350) lands on day 19, about two-thirds of the way through a window that runs from day 8 to day 26, and the trophy (500) lands on day 23. In `season.test.ts`'s three seeds, the bot reaches the wreath on days 16–18 and the trophy on days 18–20.
