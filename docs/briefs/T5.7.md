## Behavior

- Whenever `pendingDiscoveries` isn't empty and no card is showing, show a card for its first item: "New discovery!", the item's art large (at least 96 px), its name, its chain's name and tier, and Grandma's note when there is one. One button, "Add to Recipe Book", dispatches `{ type: 'dismissDiscovery', itemId }`; the next pending item's card follows.
- The card sits over the board with a dimmed backdrop, so a tap can't fall through to a board item. It doesn't close on a backdrop tap: the button is the one way on.
- Check after every store notification and once on mount (a save loaded with pending discoveries shows them straight away).
- Reduced motion: follow `prefers-reduced-motion` and fade the card in; otherwise it pops in with a short scale-up. T5.9 later adds a setting.
- Import discoveryCard.css; use the tokens; fit a 320 px wide screen.
