# Task T2.9: Merge and sell animations

Context: Rise & Shine Bakery, a browser merge game (TypeScript, Vite, PixiJS 8, Vitest).

Goal: Make merges pop and sold items burst into crumbs, with calm versions under reduced motion.

Files to create or edit: src/render/tween.ts, src/render/tween.test.ts, src/render/effects.ts, src/main.ts (one line). Do not edit any other file.
Contract:

```ts
// tween.ts — no new dependencies; driven by a PixiJS Ticker
export function easeOutBack(t: number): number;
export function easeOutCubic(t: number): number;
/**
 * Calls onUpdate with eased progress from 0 to 1 over durationMs on the ticker, then

  ticker: Ticker,
```

Effects (subscribe after the board view, so its redraw has already made the new sprites):

- `merged`: for each cell in `cells`, squash-and-pop the cell's sprite (from `board.spriteAt`): start at 0.6 scale, squashed wider than tall, overshoot to 1.15, and settle at its normal scale over 280 ms with `easeOutBack`. Add a burst of 6 small butter-yellow sparkles from the cell center in `board.effectsLayer`, fading over 300 ms.
- `sold`: a burst of 10 small crust-brown crumbs from the center of `state.lastSale.cell`, flying out and falling with light gravity, fading over 400 ms.
- Reduced motion: check `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches` for each event. Merge: fade the sprite's alpha from 0 to 1 over 150 ms, no scaling or sparkles. Sell: one crumb-colored circle fading out in place over 200 ms.
- Sprites can be replaced by a later redraw mid-animation: check `sprite.destroyed` every frame and stop. Destroy particles when they finish.

In src/main.ts, add below `// UI mounts`: `createEffects(app, boardView, store);` (use the names main.ts already has).
