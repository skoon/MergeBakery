# Task T14.4: The season lifecycle in core

Context: Rise & Shine Bakery, a browser merge game (TypeScript, Vite, PixiJS, Vitest).

Goal: a player can join a loaded season while its window is open; while it runs they tap its generator, fill seasonal orders for Season Points, and claim milestones and keepsakes; when the window closes everything seasonal is swept away and a result card is left. ## Rules

**Joining** (`joinSeason { seasonId }`):

- `seasonNotOpen` when the season isn't in `data.seasons`, `now` is outside its `startsAt`–`endsAt`, or the tutorial isn't done.
- `alreadyCompleted` when a season is already joined.
- Otherwise records `season = { seasonId, joinedAt: now, startedAt: null, endsAt: <the loaded window's endsAt>, points: 0, claimedMilestones: [], custom: null }` and emits `seasonJoined`, then tries to start it at once.

**Starting** (in `joinSeason`, then every tick until it works): when no MegaBun event is running and the season's generator fits on the board or in the Pantry (`placeGenerator`, as events do), `startedAt = now`, the generator is placed, and `seasonStarted` plus the placement events are emitted. Otherwise it waits and retries each tick, like an event waiting for room.

**While it runs** (joined and started), each tick tops seasonal orders up to `orders.maxOpen`, built like event orders: a walk-in asking for `minItems`–`maxItems` items picked evenly from the pack's ingredient and baked items within `minTier`–`maxTier`, paying the items' sell value in coins, no stars or XP, and `pointsPerItem` × item count in `seasonPoints`. They sit beside the regular orders and don't take their slots.

**Milestones** (`claimSeasonMilestone { index }`): `noActiveSeason` with no joined season, `alreadyCompleted` if claimed, `milestoneNotReached` below its points, and an out-of-range index throws. Pays its coins and gems; a keepsake adds a `KeepsakeRecord` (id, season, name, spriteKey, year) and emits `keepsakeEarned` after `seasonMilestoneClaimed`.

**MegaBun events:** no event starts while a season is joined. One that comes due meanwhile starts on the first tick after the season ends. An event already running when the player joins finishes normally, and the season starts after it.

**The end** (the first tick at or after the season's `endsAt`), all at once:

- Seasonal items on the board and in the Pantry are removed, paying their sell value (the generator pays 0). A bake of a seasonal recipe is removed from its oven, paying its output's sell value.
- Seasonal orders are removed. `lastSale` is cleared if it was a seasonal item, so undo can't bring one back.
- Seasonal ids leave `discovered`, `pendingDiscoveries` and `rewardedChains`. Staff assigned to a seasonal generator chain or recipe are unassigned.
- Reached but unclaimed milestones are paid, keepsakes included (each with `keepsakeEarned`).
- `seasonResult = { seasonId, points, coins }`, with `coins` from the sweep as for events, `season = null`, and `seasonEnded` is emitted. `dismissSeasonResult` clears the card.

A season that was still waiting to start when its window closed ends the same way, with 0 points. A save whose season is missing from `data.seasons` drops it quietly (season set to null, seasonal orders removed), as events do; T14.6 sweeps the items themselves while loading.

## Decisions for Scott

Each is a default copied from how MegaBun events work. Say if you want otherwise.

1. **Seasonal orders pay coins and Season Points only**, with no stars or XP, like event orders. Stars stay tied to the town's renovation.
2. **The Recipe Book resets each year.** Seasonal discoveries and the chain-completion gems are cleared at the end, so next year's season can pay those gems again.
3. **"For your orders" doesn't sell seasonal items.** Seasonal orders are filled from the seasonal generator, as event orders are.
4. **Joining with a full board succeeds.** The generator arrives at the first tick with room, as an event's does; the season UI (T14.8) shows "make room".
5. **A keepsake's year is the year its season started.** A December–January season is labeled with its December. Core works this out from the season's end time and window with `Date.UTC`, a pure calculation; core still never reads the clock.
