# Task T5.5: Chapter 1 dialogue

Context: Rise & Shine Bakery, a browser merge game (TypeScript, Vite, PixiJS, Vitest).
Read first: the GDD in docs/ (Overview, Orders and customers, Competitive events' "tone rules", Progression), src/ui/dialogue.ts and src/ui/dialoguePlayer.ts (T5.4), src/data/dialogue/sample.json, src/data/chapter1.json (T5.1), src/data/customers.json, src/main.ts, docs/briefs/working-rules.md.

Goal: Chapter 1 tells its story: arriving at Grandma's shop, meeting the four regulars, finding her first recipe page, and reopening day.

Starts after T5.1 and T5.4. Scott approves the script.

Files to create or edit: src/data/dialogue/chapter1.json, src/data/chapter1.json (`introSceneId` and task `sceneId`s only), src/ui/dialogue.test.ts (add tests), src/main.ts. Do not edit any other file.

## The scenes

| Scene id          | Plays when                                       | About                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `ch1-intro`       | a new game starts (the chapter's `introSceneId`) | The player arrives at the dusty shop; Grandma's letter, read aloud, leaves them the bakery and hints her recipes are scattered. A first hint of MegaBun's interest in the lot. |
| `ch1-gus`         | `wash-window` completes                          | Gus the Fisherman spots the clean window and becomes the first regular.                                                                                                        |
| `ch1-edith`       | `fix-stool` completes                            | Miss Edith, the retired teacher, remembers Grandma's croissants.                                                                                                               |
| `ch1-dex`         | `shop-bell` completes                            | Dex, the MegaBun spy, "just browsing", clearly moved by the smell of bread.                                                                                                    |
| `ch1-mina`        | `mend-awning` completes                          | Mina, a neighbor who wants the shop to thrive.                                                                                                                                 |
| `ch1-recipe-page` | `first-recipe-page` completes                    | The player finds Grandma's first recipe page, and a note in her hand.                                                                                                          |
| `ch1-reopening`   | `reopening-day` completes                        | The doors open; the regulars come; MegaBun sends a comically corporate "congratulations".                                                                                      |

Set `introSceneId` and those tasks' `sceneId`s in chapter1.json; every other task keeps `sceneId: null`.

**Tone** (GDD): warm, funny, underdog comedy. MegaBun is huge, rich and clueless, never menacing; jokes punch up at corporate habits, not at its staff. Grandma is kind, a little mischievous, and present only through her letters and notes.

**Length:** 4–10 lines per scene, each line at most 140 characters so it fits the box on a 320 px screen. Speakers are the customers above, `grandma` for her letters and notes, and `narrator`. Use each regular's `neutral` and `happy` expressions; `impatient` sparingly.

## Playing them

In main.ts: load and parse `src/data/dialogue/chapter1.json` with `parseScenes`, mount the dialogue player, then:

- on a new game only (no save was loaded), play the chapter's `introSceneId` once the UI has mounted;
- on every `taskCompleted` event whose task has a `sceneId`, play that scene. The player queues, so a scene that arrives while another plays waits its turn.

Rules: no edits to types.ts; follow docs/briefs/working-rules.md.

Done when: `npx vitest run src/ui/dialogue.test.ts` passes, with added tests (using `loadGameData()`) for: chapter1.json parsing; `missingSceneIds` returning `[]` for the real chapter and scenes; every scene 4–10 lines; every line at most 140 characters. `npm run build` succeeds (delete dist/ afterwards). The checks in working-rules.md pass. Scott approves the script, and the phase-end review checks in a browser that the intro plays on a new game and not on a reload, and each regular's scene plays when their task completes.
If the contract seems wrong, stop and explain instead of changing it.
