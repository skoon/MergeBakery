# Task T14.11: Seasonal orders the player can fill, and the season's recipes in its sheet

Context: Rise & Shine Bakery, a browser merge game (TypeScript, Vite, PixiJS, Vitest).
Read first: `src/core/seasons.ts` (`generateSeasonOrder`, `topUpOrders`), `src/core/orders.ts` (`producibleChains`), `src/core/seasonHooks.ts` (`seasonSpawnTable`), `src/ui/kitchenModel.ts` (`recipeAvailable`), `src/ui/seasonModel.ts` and `seasonSheet.ts`, `docs/briefs/T14.9.md`, docs/briefs/working-rules.md.

Goal: a T14.10 "fix now" finding. Scott, Nov 10: "I don't know how to bake the new seasonal items." Recipes are listed in one place only, the Oven's Kitchen sheet. That sheet hides a recipe until the player can get every input. Pumpkin pie needs an egg pair from a Hen Coop, and Cranberry tart a sugar bowl from a Sugar Tin. Both generators arrive in Chapter 2, but the season can be joined right after the tutorial. A Chapter 1 player therefore sees neither recipe, yet seasonal orders ask for pies and tarts. Those orders can't be filled, and they fill the three order slots. The simulator missed it because the bot has finished every chapter by the time the window opens.

The fix (Scott chose option A, Nov 10):

- Seasonal orders ask only for what the player can make now.
- The season sheet lists the season's recipes and their ingredients, and says what's missing.

Branch: `t14.11-makeable-orders` (off `t14.9-thanksgiving`). Built in the same uncommitted working tree as T14.7–T14.9; the Files table is the split for the commit.

## What to build

**Orders.** `generateSeasonOrder` picks from the pack's ingredient and baked items within its tiers, as now, but only from those the player can make now (decision 1). If the pack has no such items at all, that's still a programming error and throws. If it has some but none can be made right now, no order arrives, and the next tick tries again.

**Recipes in the sheet.** `SeasonSheetModel` gains `recipes`: one entry for each of the season's recipes, in data order. Each entry has:

- the name, output art and bake time, as the Kitchen sheet shows them;
- each input's name and art;
- `needs`: null when the recipe is available, else text such as "Needs a Hen Coop" (decision 4).

The sheet shows a "Recipes" section between the how-to text and the track, in every state: offer, waiting and running. It reuses the Kitchen sheet's recipe look where it can. Missing inputs are shown dimmed, with the `needs` line under the recipe.

## Decisions (approved by Scott, Nov 10)

1. **What "can make now" means.**
   - An ingredient item counts when a generator the player owns can spawn its chain. Owned means on the board (not cobwebbed) or in the Pantry. The count includes the season's `extraSpawns`, so pecans count once any Flour Mill is owned.
   - A baked item counts when the recipe that makes its chain is available. That's the Kitchen sheet's rule: every input is held or its chain is spawned by an owned generator. A pie pair counts once pies can be baked.
   - Undiscovered items are still allowed. Seasonal orders are how players meet the season's items, which is different from regular orders.
2. **`recipeAvailable` moves to `core/orders.ts`.** Core can't import from `ui/`. Next to `producibleChains`, which it already uses, it adds no import cycle. It would add one in `kitchen.ts`, which imports from `seasons.ts`. The three files that import it are updated rather than re-exported, so it has a single home.
3. **The `extraSpawns` check makes `seasons.ts` import `seasonSpawnTable` from `seasonHooks.ts`.** `seasonHooks.ts` already imports `runningSeason` from `seasons.ts`, so this makes an import cycle.
   - The cycle is safe: neither module uses the other while loading, only inside functions.
   - The alternative is moving `runningSeason` to a module of its own. That touches three files and changes no behaviour.
   - The check calls the pack's `extraSpawns` with the dispatch's `rng`, as a tap does. A hook that draws from it changes the draws that follow, deterministically.
4. **"Needs a …" names a generator.** For each missing input, name the lowest-tier generator whose spawn table holds the input's chain ("Hen Coop", "Sugar Tin"), joined with "and". If no generator spawns it, name the input itself ("Needs a Flour bag"). The chapter isn't named, because generators also come from the Shop. Added Nov 11 (Scott): until the season starts, what its own generator spawns counts as available, neither dimmed nor named, since the generator arrives when the season starts. Before joining, the sheet says "Needs a Hen Coop", not "Needs a Pumpkin Patch and a Hen Coop".
5. **The Kitchen sheet is unchanged.** It still lists only what can be baked now. The season sheet is where a player learns the season.
6. **Orders already open aren't touched.** No save change. Only dev saves have orders the player can't make. `bakery.give('hen-coop-1', 'sugar-tin-1')` fills them, or `bakery.endSeason()` and joining again resets them.

## Tests

- `orders.test.ts`: the moved `recipeAvailable` tests, unchanged apart from the import.
- `seasons.test.ts` (autumn fixture: Gourd pie needs a flour bag and a butter pat):
  - With no Dairy Fridge, many top-ups never ask for the pie chain.
  - With a Dairy Fridge in the Pantry, the pie chain appears.
  - A cobwebbed generator doesn't count.
  - Nothing makeable means no order and no throw.
  - A season item spawned only through `extraSpawns` is asked for while its generator is owned and never without it.
- `rules.test.ts` (real Thanksgiving pack):
  - A Chapter 1 board (Pumpkin Patch, Flour Mill, Dairy Fridge) gets only pumpkin, cranberry and pecan items over many top-ups.
  - Adding a Hen Coop brings pies.
  - Adding a Sugar Tin brings tarts.
  - With no Flour Mill there are no pecans.
- `seasonModel.test.ts`:
  - Recipes are listed in every state.
  - `needs` is null when the recipe is available.
  - "Needs a Hen Coop" when only eggs are missing; both generators named when both are missing.
  - A held input counts as available.
